## Roadmap interpretation

1/3/5/10 Year는 exact delivery promise가 아니라 outcome horizon이다. calendar start, funding, adoption, legal/policy와 pilot evidence에 따라 CR/decision으로 변경한다. detailed development phase는 Book 12가 source of truth다.

## Platform evolution

`Internal Platform → Verified AI MLS → Governed Broker Network → Potential National Platform`

각 단계는 이전 단계의 control/evidence를 유지하며 자동으로 다음 단계로 진행하지 않는다.

## 1 Year — Internal platform

### Intended outcomes

- staff가 manual intake부터 search, shortlist, verification와 controlled proposal을 하나의 trace로 수행
- BG-001–BG-006 baseline과 pilot evidence 확보
- property/unit/offer/source separation과 duplicate workflow 검증
- zero unauthorized publication, complete provenance/permission audit target

### Exit evidence

- internal adoption과 measurable time/rework improvement
- security/privacy/retention/AI evaluation gate 통과
- reliable manual fallback와 operational ownership
- Book 0–12/F1 architecture freeze 및 controlled pilot approval

## 3 Year — AI MLS operation

### Intended outcomes

- multiple approved source/intake path와 provider-independent AI assistance
- recurring verification/freshness, source contribution와 business analytics
- selected developer/owner/broker integration pilot
- repeatable onboarding, support, incident와 quality governance

### Gate

- internal KPI improvement와 trust controls가 stable
- source policy/data rights와 partner authority validated
- enterprise/API/analytics hypothesis별 business case

## 5 Year — Broker network

### POST-MVP intended outcomes

- verified external broker identity/membership and contribution
- shared data vocabulary, update/correction/dispute and enforcement rules
- permission-controlled cross-broker cooperation
- sustainable membership/enterprise/analytics model 중 evidence-backed option

### Gate

- network value가 participant cost/risk를 초과한다는 pilot evidence
- governance independence, sanctions/appeal와 data portability
- privacy/security/legal and professional-practice review

## 10 Year — Potential national platform

### Vision, not commitment

- multiple regions/segments에서 interoperable property intelligence/cooperation
- national-scale identifiers/standards와 local variation support
- reliable market/demand insight with privacy-safe aggregation
- developer, broker, owner와 customer ecosystem에서 trusted infrastructure role

### Non-negotiable condition

“national”은 coverage claim이 아니라 governance, quality, interoperability와 measurable trust가 광범위하게 검증된 결과여야 한다. public utility, regulator-like authority 또는 market completeness를 승인 없이 주장하지 않는다.

## Horizon matrix

| Horizon | Primary user | Core value | Business model | Governance maturity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Year | internal staff | productivity + verified proposal | brokerage improvement | internal approved controls |
| 3 Year | staff + selected partners | scalable intelligence/integration | internal + validated pilots | partner/source policy |
| 5 Year | governed broker members | contribution/cooperation network | membership/enterprise hypotheses | membership/enforcement/dispute |
| 10 Year | broader ecosystem | interoperable trusted platform | evidence-based portfolio | multi-region/national governance |

## Roadmap review triggers

- constitutional amendment 또는 regulatory/platform policy change
- KPI control failure/unauthorized exposure
- invalidated market/adoption/business-model assumption
- funding/operational capacity change
- partner/network pilot result
- architecture/security review finding

## Stop/go rule

각 horizon은 value KPI, control KPI, risk, capability ownership과 rollback/exit가 승인되어야 시작한다. 더 먼 horizon의 feature를 현재 scope 정당화에 사용하지 않는다.

> **OPEN DECISION:** roadmap clock start, geographic/segment priority, investment envelope와 horizon owner는 A13 전에 확정한다.
