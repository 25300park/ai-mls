## Model status

current model은 internal productivity value다. future revenue 항목은 모두 `HYPOTHESIS` 또는 `POST-MVP`이며 pricing, demand, legality나 feasibility가 검증된 commitment가 아니다.

## Current business model

AI MLS는 mrHOMES brokerage operation을 지원하는 internal capability다. 가치는 별도 software revenue보다 다음 operational/economic effect로 시작한다.

- search/re-entry/rework에 쓰는 staff time 감소
- first shortlist와 client response 개선
- verified option과 consistent proposal로 trust/viewing opportunity 향상
- source contribution visibility를 통한 effort allocation
- unauthorized publication/privacy/data risk 감소

## Future business model

future model은 internal operating advantage를 유지하면서 approved partner/network capability를 단계적으로 추가한다. 각 모델은 separate business case, governance, pricing, support, data right와 security review를 통과해야 한다.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brokerage improvement | Current/MVP | mrHOMES internal | productivity, conversion, risk reduction | KPI baseline/pilot |
| Broker membership | POST-MVP | external brokers | governed contribution, reach, shared tools | membership/rule/network proof |
| Enterprise subscription | POST-MVP | brokerages/developers | private intelligence workflow | multi-org/privacy/support proof |

## Membership

membership는 data access fee가 아니라 identity, contribution authority, quality/update obligation, permission, audit, enforcement, dispute와 termination rule을 포함해야 한다. member-submitted data라고 해서 자동 verified/publishable하지 않는다.

## Enterprise

enterprise hypothesis는 organization boundary, tenant isolation, role delegation, audit ownership, data export/retention와 support SLA를 요구한다. MVP internal design을 그대로 판매 가능한 product라고 가정하지 않는다.

## API

API revenue는 final API가 존재한다는 가정이 아니다. future access는 licensing, purpose limitation, field-level authorization, rate/abuse control, audit, version/support와 revocation capability가 준비된 경우에만 검토한다.

## Analytics

analytics는 operational improvement부터 시작한다. contact/client 개인 수준의 monetization을 금지하고 aggregation, minimization, consent/legal basis, re-identification risk와 data quality를 review한다.

## Future marketplace

marketplace는 10-year possibility이며 supply/demand listing board와 다르다. transaction/intermediation role, professional regulation, payment, dispute, fraud, consumer protection와 governance가 정의되기 전에는 scope에 넣지 않는다.

## Business model decision gates

- validated customer/problem and willingness-to-pay evidence
- constitutional/source-policy/privacy/security compliance
- unit economics and support/operation feasibility
- data quality/coverage and partner authority
- reversible pilot with explicit success/failure criteria
- ARB/Business/User Approval and separate roadmap phase

> **OPEN DECISION:** pricing, cost baseline, revenue target와 legal/tax treatment는 future business case에서 검증한다.
